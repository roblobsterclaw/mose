## Must do

- [ ] **1. File Form 706 to elect portability of Keli's unused estate-tax exemption.**
  Not probate, and no tax is owed. It transfers Keli's unused federal exemption
  (~$15M) to Joe, roughly doubling his. Who: CPA or estate attorney, simplified
  portability-only return. Deadline: 9 months after date of death, 6-month
  extension available, and a simplified late election is allowed up to 5 years
  after death for estates not otherwise required to file. Added by Joe 2026-09-02.
