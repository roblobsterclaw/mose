🎯 MOSE Verdict

Meta owns the largest attention network ever assembled — Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Messenger — ~4B people, monetized by a world-class AI-driven ad engine. After the 2022 "year of efficiency" reset, it became a cash machine again, and the MOSE model now sees it as the cheapest of the mega-cap quality names: ~20% upside to a $682 intrinsic value at just ~24x earnings on ~20% growth. The conviction shows: Mohnish Pabrai has it at 28%, Brad Gerstner 20%, Bill Ackman 11%, plus Coleman and Niles.

Action: BUY CANDIDATE / accumulate. Quality, growth, AND a margin of safety to intrinsic value — a rare combination among the giants. The offset is Reality Labs spend (the metaverse money pit) and AI-capex intensity, but the core ad business funds it many times over. A core holding to build.

🏰 Business & Moat

Meta is a four-app network monopoly on social attention with a self-improving ad machine: more users → more data → better targeting → higher ad ROI → more advertisers. AI deepens this (Advantage+, recommendation engines, Llama). Optionality from WhatsApp/business messaging, Reels monetization, and (speculatively) AI agents and AR glasses. The moat is scale + network effects + the best ads infrastructure in the industry.

📈 Bull Case

  • ~24x earnings, ~20% growth, ~20% upside to IV — cheapest quality mega-cap.
  • AI is a direct ad-revenue tailwind (targeting + content engagement).
  • Buybacks + a new dividend; fortress balance sheet.
  • WhatsApp/business messaging and Reels are under-monetized optionality.

📉 Risks

  • Reality Labs burns tens of billions with uncertain payoff.
  • AI capex is enormous; returns must materialize.
  • Regulatory/antitrust and privacy (Apple ATT-style) headwinds.
  • Ad cyclicality and platform/competition risk (TikTok).
