🎯 MOSE Verdict

Alibaba is China's dominant e-commerce + cloud franchise (Taobao/Tmall, Alibaba Cloud, Cainiao, international commerce), trading at a deep discount to US peers on a low-teens earnings multiple with a huge net-cash position. The cloud arm is a real AI beneficiary in China.

⚠️ Model caveat: the raw MOSE DCF prints an inflated IV (>$700) driven by unreliable inputs on a foreign ADR; it is not usable as a price target. Qualitatively the stock screens cheap — but the gap between cheapness and value is the China risk discount: VIE ownership structure, regulatory unpredictability, and US-listing/geopolitical overhang.

Action: SPECULATIVE VALUE. Statistically cheap with real assets and AI-cloud optionality, but this is a position-size-it-small, eyes-open China bet — not a margin-of-safety compounder in the Buffett sense.

🏰 Business & Moat

Alibaba's moat is scale in Chinese commerce (Taobao/Tmall network effects), the #1 cloud platform in China (an AI tailwind via model hosting and compute), logistics (Cainiao), and a fintech affiliate (Ant). The business is strong; the discount is almost entirely about who controls it and under what rules.

📈 Bull Case

  • ~14x earnings + large net cash — statistically cheap.
  • Alibaba Cloud is a leading China AI/compute beneficiary.
  • Buybacks shrinking the share count meaningfully.
  • Dominant commerce franchise with optionality (Ant, intl).

📉 Risks

  • VIE structure — foreign holders own a contract, not the assets.
  • Chinese regulatory and US-delisting/geopolitical risk.
  • Model IV is broken — do not anchor on it.
  • Domestic competition (PDD, Douyin) pressuring growth.
