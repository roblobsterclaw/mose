🎯 MOSE Verdict

Progressive is the gold-standard P&C auto insurer — decades of disciplined underwriting, a structural data/pricing advantage (telematics, segmentation), and a combined ratio consistently below peers. It grows premiums double-digits while underwriting at a profit, a rare combination. It trades at only ~11.5x earnings, and the model sees real upside to intrinsic value.

⚠️ Model caveat: the raw MOSE DCF prints an implausibly high IV (>$700) because the model annualizes a hard-market spike in earnings. Normalized, intrinsic value is more reasonably in the ~$280-320 range — still above the ~$220 price, supporting the constructive call without taking the raw number literally.

Action: BUY CANDIDATE. Quality compounder, low multiple, structural moat. The cleanest value-meets-quality name in this batch.

👥 Super-Investor Backing

Held across quality-insurance books

A favored compounder among insurance-focused quality investors; the combination of growth + underwriting discipline + low P/E is unusual.

🏰 Business & Moat

Progressive's moat is information: the most granular pricing/segmentation data in US auto, refined over decades, which lets it price risk more accurately than rivals and still grow. Direct + agency distribution, a trusted brand, and a culture obsessed with the combined ratio (target 96). Scale compounds the data advantage.

📈 Bull Case

  • ~11.5x earnings on a double-digit grower — rare value.
  • Structural underwriting/pricing edge vs peers.
  • Hard auto-insurance market boosting margins and growth.
  • Long reinvestment run. high ROE, disciplined culture.

📉 Risks

  • Insurance earnings are cyclical; the hard market will soften.
  • Raw model IV is overstated — use normalized figures.
  • Catastrophe/severity (inflation in repair costs) risk.
  • Competition from GEICO and tech-forward insurers.
