🎯 MOSE Verdict

Moody's is one half of the global credit-ratings duopoly (with S&P) — a regulatory-blessed tollbooth on the world's debt issuance, plus a fast-growing analytics arm (Moody's Analytics). Pricing power is extraordinary and incremental margins are huge. It's a quintessential Akre/Buffett "compounding machine," which is why Dev Kantesaria has it at 17%, Chris Hohn 14%, Chuck Akre 9%, and Buffett still owns 4% (a decades-long Berkshire holding).

Action: high-quality watchlist hold; accumulate on weakness. At ~$450 it's ~22% above our base value of $369 and far above the $277 MOS price — no cushion today. The ratings business is mildly cyclical with debt-issuance volumes; the best entry points come in credit-market scares. Hold if owned; add on a meaningful pullback.

🏰 Business & Moat

Two engines: Moody's Investors Service (ratings — the toll on debt issuance, oligopoly economics, regulatory entrenchment) and Moody's Analytics (subscription data, risk, and software — recurring, sticky, faster-growing). The moat is reputation + regulation + the simple fact that issuers need a rating to access capital markets cheaply. Few businesses convert revenue to owner earnings as efficiently.

📈 Bull Case

  • Duopoly pricing power; ratings revenue compounds with global debt.
  • Analytics shifting the mix toward recurring, higher-multiple revenue.
  • Asset-light, high-margin, prodigious free cash flow + buybacks.
  • Private-credit boom expands the addressable issuance base.

📉 Risks

  • No margin of safety ~22% above base IV at 32× earnings.
  • Issuance cyclicality — high rates / frozen credit markets hit ratings volume.
  • Regulatory or litigation risk to the ratings model.
  • Smaller, more rate-sensitive than the payments names.
