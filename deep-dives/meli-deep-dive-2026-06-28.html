🎯 MOSE Verdict

MercadoLibre is the Amazon + PayPal + Shopify of Latin America — the dominant e-commerce marketplace and a fast-scaling fintech (Mercado Pago) across a region with rising internet penetration and a large underbanked population. It's the rare name here growing revenue ~25% while our owner-earnings model still sees ~8% upside to a $1,998 intrinsic value — a genuine (if modest) margin of safety on a high-quality grower.

Action: BUY CANDIDATE / accumulate. Unlike the mega-caps priced for perfection, MELI offers growth and a small valuation cushion. The trade-off is higher volatility (emerging-market FX, macro) and a ~43× multiple that demands continued execution. A core "20-year compounder" candidate for the growth sleeve; scale in, expect a bumpy ride.

🏰 Business & Moat

Three reinforcing engines: Marketplace (the #1 e-commerce platform in Brazil, Mexico, Argentina and beyond), Mercado Pago (digital payments + credit + a fast-growing fintech/banking stack), and Mercado Envíos (its own logistics network). The moat is a local flywheel — selection, logistics, and payments compounding in markets where incumbents are weak and Amazon is a distant challenger. Fintech is the kicker: serving the underbanked is an enormous, high-margin TAM.

📈 Bull Case

  • ~25% revenue growth with a long runway — low LatAm e-commerce/fintech penetration.
  • Marketplace + payments + logistics + credit flywheel widening the moat.
  • Fintech (Mercado Pago) could become the larger, higher-margin business.
  • Modest margin of safety to intrinsic value — rare for a grower this fast.

📉 Risks

  • Emerging-market risk: FX devaluation, inflation, political/macro shocks.
  • ~43× earnings — execution must stay excellent or it derates hard.
  • Rising credit book adds risk if LatAm consumer credit sours.
  • Competition from Amazon, Shopee/Sea, and local players.
