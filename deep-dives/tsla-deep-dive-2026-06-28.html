🎯 MOSE Verdict

Tesla is the dominant Western EV maker and, increasingly, an AI/robotics bet — autonomy (FSD/robotaxi), energy storage, and the Optimus humanoid. The valuation is no longer about cars: at ~120x+ earnings and a ~$1.3T market cap, the stock prices in massive success in businesses that are still largely pre-revenue.

⚠️ Model caveat: the MOSE owner-earnings DCF prints a near-zero intrinsic value (≈$17) because it values Tesla as an auto manufacturer on current earnings, ignoring the autonomy/energy optionality the market is paying for. So the raw IV understates the bull case as badly as the price overstates the proven business. The honest read: unanalyzable by margin-of-safety methods — it is a venture-style bet on FSD/robotaxi/Optimus, not a value investment.

Action: PASS on value grounds / WATCH as a thematic. No margin of safety exists at any reasonable discount rate on current fundamentals; ownership is a conviction call on autonomy, not on the numbers.

🏰 Business & Moat

Tesla's assets are a leading EV brand, the best-scaled charging network, a strong energy-storage business, and a real lead in real-world driving data for autonomy. The bull case is that FSD/robotaxi and Optimus turn it into an AI platform. The bear case is that it remains a (cyclical, competitive) car company priced like a software monopoly.

📈 Bull Case

  • Real lead in autonomy data and energy storage.
  • Robotaxi/FSD and Optimus are enormous TAMs if they work.
  • Strong brand, vertical integration, charging network.
  • Optionality the DCF cannot capture.

📉 Risks

  • ~120x+ earnings — extreme valuation on proven business.
  • Both model IV (~$17) and price are unreliable anchors.
  • EV competition + margin pressure + demand cyclicality.
  • Autonomy timelines repeatedly slip; key-person risk.
