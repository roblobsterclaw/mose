🎯 MOSE Verdict

Capital One is a data-and-technology-led consumer bank (cards + lending) that just absorbed Discover — giving it a payments network of its own, not just an issuer position. The MOSE model flags a genuine value setup: ~34% upside to a $306 intrinsic value, and it is held by sharp investors — Clifford Sosin (CAS) 6%, Glenn Greenberg 4.5%, and even Buffett.

Action: ACCUMULATE — one of the few names here with a real margin of safety. The thesis is the Discover integration: owning the network captures interchange economics and removes a big cost, potentially re-rating the multiple. The risk is credit — Capital One lends to a more subprime-tilted base, so a recession bites harder. Sized appropriately, an attractive value holding.

🏰 Business & Moat

Capital One pioneered data-driven underwriting in credit cards and has scaled into a top-tier consumer bank. The Discover acquisition is transformational: it adds a fourth U.S. card network (alongside Visa/Mastercard/Amex), letting Capital One keep more of the transaction economics and build a differentiated rewards ecosystem. Moat = data/underwriting edge + brand + (now) network ownership.

📈 Bull Case

  • ~34% discount to intrinsic value — a rare margin of safety.
  • Discover network ownership = structural margin + re-rating optionality.
  • Strong underwriting/data culture; buybacks below book-value-plus.
  • Backed by Sosin, Greenberg, Buffett.

📉 Risks

  • Credit cycle: subprime-tilted book is recession-sensitive.
  • Integration risk on a very large acquisition.
  • Regulatory scrutiny of the deal and of card practices.
  • Banks are cyclical and capital-intensive — not a "set and forget."
