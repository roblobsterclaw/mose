🎯 MOSE Verdict

S&P Global is the other half of the ratings duopoly — plus it owns the S&P 500 and Dow Jones indices (a royalty on the ETF boom), S&P Capital IQ data, Platts commodity benchmarks, and Mobility (auto data). It's arguably a higher-quality, more-diversified version of Moody's. And notably, it's the one ratings name trading right at our intrinsic value — base value $417 vs ~$406 price.

Action: ACCUMULATE. Of the three "tollbooth" compounders (Visa/Moody's/S&P), SPGI offers the only real margin-of-safety-friendly entry today — essentially fair value with multiple growth engines. Dev Kantesaria has made it a 22% top position alongside Chris Hohn (13%). A core quality holding to build on weakness.

🏰 Business & Moat

Four moaty franchises under one roof: Ratings (duopoly tollbooth on debt), Indices (the S&P 500 brand — a near-pure royalty that scales with passive investing), Market Intelligence (Capital IQ data subscriptions), and Commodity/Mobility (Platts, auto data). The indices business alone is one of the best on the market — owners of a benchmark collect fees on trillions in linked assets with almost no cost. Diversification makes SPGI less issuance-cyclical than pure-play Moody's.

📈 Bull Case

  • Trading at fair value with several compounding engines.
  • Indices = a royalty on the unstoppable shift to passive/ETFs.
  • Data + ratings + benchmarks: diversified, recurring, high-margin.
  • Strong buybacks; reliable double-digit earnings growth.

📉 Risks

  • Ratings cyclicality (shared with Moody's) in frozen credit markets.
  • Still ~28× earnings — quality is priced in; downturns can derate it.
  • Integration/execution across many segments (post-IHS Markit).
  • Index competition and fee pressure at the margin.
