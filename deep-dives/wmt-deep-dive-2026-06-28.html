🎯 MOSE Verdict

Walmart is the largest retailer on earth and has reinvented itself into an omnichannel + advertising + marketplace flywheel (Walmart Connect ad business, Walmart+, automation). It is an extraordinary operation — but it now trades at ~42x earnings, a multiple historically reserved for software, on mid-single-digit growth. The MOSE model values the cash flows near $35, far below the ~$114 quote: overvalued by any classic value lens.

Action: WATCH. Own the quality if you already do; there is no margin of safety to add here. The market is paying a premium for the ad-business re-rating story — a lot has to go right to justify it.

👥 Super-Investor Backing

Broad institutional ownership — defensive quality staple

A core defensive holding across many quality books, but not a current high-conviction value buy among the MOSE super-investors.

🏰 Business & Moat

Unmatched scale in retail: ~10,500 stores, the deepest supply chain in the world, and now a fast-growing high-margin ad and marketplace layer that improves blended economics. The moat is cost leadership (everyday low prices) plus convenience (stores within 10 miles of ~90% of the US) — increasingly digital.

📈 Bull Case

  • Omnichannel + advertising lifts margins above pure retail.
  • Scale and logistics moat widening with automation.
  • Defensive, recession-resilient demand.
  • Share gains in grocery and higher-income shoppers.

📉 Risks

  • ~42x P/E on ~5% growth — priced for perfection.
  • Model IV ~$35 implies large overvaluation if growth slows.
  • Thin retail margins; any miss is punished.
  • Tariff/import-cost and consumer-spend sensitivity.
