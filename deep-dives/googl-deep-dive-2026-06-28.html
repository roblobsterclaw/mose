🎯 MOSE Verdict

Alphabet is the single highest-conviction name in the MOSE universe — 26 of the tracked super-investors own it, led by Li Lu (Himalaya Capital), who has made GOOGL his largest position at ~22–23% of the portfolio. The business is a wide-moat cash machine trading at a still-reasonable ~31× trailing earnings despite owning the most valuable real estate on the internet and one of the three leading frontier-AI labs.

The catch for a margin-of-safety buyer: at ~$337 the stock sits only ~9% below our base owner-earnings intrinsic value of $372, and above the 25%-margin "fat-pitch" entry of ~$279. So it's a quality-at-fair-price accumulate, not a screaming bargain. Action: core long-term hold; add aggressively on any pullback toward $280–$300 (a ~15% dip), where the margin of safety becomes compelling.

Durability test (the "20-year question")

Will people still search, watch video, run cloud workloads, and want AI assistants in 20 years? Almost certainly yes — and Alphabet owns leading assets in all four. The moat is among the widest in the market: brand, data flywheels, distribution control, and a self-funded R&D budget few can match.

📈 Bull Case

  • AI is a tailwind, not just a threat. Gemini is a top-tier frontier model; Alphabet owns the full stack — chips (TPUs), data centers, models, and global distribution to 2B+ users. AI Overviews and Gemini integration deepen the Search moat rather than erode it.
  • Cloud inflection. Google Cloud has flipped to durable profitability and is compounding 25–30%+ on AI demand — a second growth engine the market still under-credits.
  • Waymo optionality. The robotaxi lead is real and scaling city by city — essentially a free call option at the current multiple.
  • Capital returns + fortress balance sheet. Massive buybacks, a growing dividend, and a net-cash position. Owner earnings (~$10.81 TTM EPS, growing low-teens) comfortably fund record AI capex.
  • Cheapest of the "Magnificent" cohort on quality-adjusted earnings, despite arguably the best assets.

📉 Bear Case / Risks

  • Antitrust overhang. The DOJ search case (remedies phase) and the ad-tech case are genuine tail risks — forced changes to default-search deals (the Apple arrangement) or an ad-tech divestiture would dent economics. Base case is fines/behavioral remedies, not a breakup, but it's the #1 watch item.
  • AI disruption to Search. If users shift queries to chat-style assistants (ChatGPT, etc.), the highest-margin ad inventory could erode faster than Cloud/AI monetization replaces it.
  • Capex intensity. AI infrastructure spend is enormous; if returns disappoint, free cash flow and margins compress.
  • Regulatory/EU pressure and the perennial "law of large numbers" — growth must decelerate eventually off a $4T base.
  • Limited margin of safety today — see valuation below.
