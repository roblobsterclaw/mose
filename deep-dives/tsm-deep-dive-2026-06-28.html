🎯 MOSE Verdict

TSMC is the company that physically manufactures the AI revolution — the dominant leading-edge foundry (3nm/2nm), with Apple, NVIDIA, AMD, and every hyperscaler dependent on its capacity. It pairs near-monopoly process leadership with strong pricing power and expanding margins as AI demand outstrips supply.

⚠️ Model caveat: the MOSE DCF returns a nonsensical intrinsic value (the model mis-scales TSMC's ADR vs the Taiwan-listed shares and the FX), so the printed IV is unusable. Judging qualitatively: at ~28x earnings for the world's most critical chipmaker growing 20-30% on the AI buildout, valuation is full but defensible — call it fair-to-attractive for a long-term holder, with the caveat below.

Action: WATCH / ACCUMULATE on weakness. The business quality is A+; the swing factor is Taiwan/China geopolitical risk, which no DCF captures.

🏰 Business & Moat

TSMC's moat is process leadership + scale + ecosystem: it is years ahead at the leading edge, and the capital intensity (~$30-40B/yr capex, EUV fleet) makes the barrier to entry almost insurmountable. Customers co-design around its process. As AI chips concentrate on the most advanced nodes, TSMC captures the value.

📈 Bull Case

  • Near-monopoly on leading-edge logic — the AI bottleneck.
  • Pricing power; margins expanding with node leadership.
  • 20-30% revenue growth driven by AI/HPC demand.
  • Diversifying fabs to Arizona/Japan/Germany.

📉 Risks

  • Taiwan geopolitical risk is the dominant tail risk.
  • Model IV is unusable — judge on multiples/qualitatively.
  • Extreme capital intensity and cyclicality.
  • Customer concentration (Apple/NVIDIA) and chip-cycle swings.
