🎯 MOSE Verdict

ASML is a literal monopoly: the only company on earth that makes EUV lithography machines, without which advanced chips (and therefore AI) cannot be built. The business quality is essentially unique. But the market knows it — ASML trades at ~62x earnings, and the MOSE model values it near $728 versus a ~$1,873 quote, flagging it richly overvalued on a margin-of-safety basis.

Action: WATCH. A wonderful business you want to own at the right price; this is not that price. Put it on the buy-the-dip list and wait for a semi-cycle scare.

👥 Super-Investor Backing

Held by AI-infrastructure and quality investors

A core long-term holding for those underwriting the multi-decade chip buildout — but most value investors wait for cyclical pullbacks to add.

🏰 Business & Moat

ASML's moat is as deep as moats get: a sole-source monopoly on EUV (and dominance in DUV), protected by decades of R&D, a global supplier network (Zeiss optics), and an installed base that generates high-margin service/upgrade revenue. Every leading-edge fab on the planet depends on its tools. High-NA EUV extends the runway.

📈 Bull Case

  • Sole-source EUV monopoly — no substitute exists.
  • Growing high-margin installed-base service revenue.
  • AI/advanced-node demand drives the order book.
  • High-NA EUV extends leadership for a decade+.

📉 Risks

  • ~62x earnings, model IV ~$728: large overvaluation.
  • Semi-equipment is deeply cyclical; orders are lumpy.
  • China export-control exposure to revenue.
  • Customer concentration (TSMC, Samsung, Intel).
