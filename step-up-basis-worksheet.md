## Form 706 notes

- Filing deadline: **April 22, 2027**; automatic 6-month extension to
  October 22, 2027 on Form 4768. Simplified late portability election
  available up to 5 years after death if the estate was not otherwise
  required to file.
- Alternate valuation date (January 22, 2027) is only available if it lowers
  the estate tax; with no tax due, use date-of-death values.
- The gross estate includes Keli's half of joint property, her IRA and Roth
  401(k), any business interest, and life insurance she owned. At this
  estate's size no tax is due; the return exists to port her unused
  exemption to Joe.
- Ownership share caution: the Tiller balance sheet carries business debt at
  100% but Joe's stake at 48.33% / 40%. Use the entity-level valuation times
  the ownership percentage, not the raw balances.
